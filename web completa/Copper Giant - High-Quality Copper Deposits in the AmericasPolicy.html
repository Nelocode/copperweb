Copper Giant is part of the Fiore Group, a private Canadian organization known for building successful, high-impact companies across the natural resource sector. Formed with a singular purpose—to advance high-quality copper projects responsibly and efficiently—the Company is led by a team with uncommon experience, having taken several major copper mines from discovery through to construction.

Copper Giant's current focus is the Mocoa copper-molybdenum project in southern Colombia, one of the largest undeveloped copper systems in the Americas. Recent results point to district-scale potential well beyond the original footprint. Guided by values of respect and responsibility, and grounded in its Good Neighbor philosophy, Copper Giant is committed to creating enduring value for all stakeholders and contributing meaningfully to the global energy transition.
