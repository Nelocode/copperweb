Copper Giant, part of the Fiore Group, is dedicated to responsibly advancing high-quality copper projects. Led by a team with extensive experience in major mine development, from discovery through construction, our current focus is the Mocoa copper-molybdenum porphyry system in southern Colombia, one of the largest undeveloped copper systems in the Americas. Grounded in our Good Neighbor Philosophy, we are committed to building long-term value for our communities while contributing to the global energy transition.
