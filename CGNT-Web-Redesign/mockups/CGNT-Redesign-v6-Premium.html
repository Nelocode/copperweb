The Making of a Giant

Copper Giant holds 100% interest in the project — a billion-tonne resource near surface in a tier-1 jurisdiction with existing infrastructure.

The scale matters in this market. With copper fundamentals tightening and a structural supply gap widening every quarter, Mocoa is positioned to be a strategic answer.
